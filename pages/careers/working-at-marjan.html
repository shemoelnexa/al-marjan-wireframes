01 The Work

A masterplan, not a project.

Marjan reports to the Government of Ras Al Khaimah and answers to a thirty-year horizon — not a fund cycle.

We are stewarding the build-out of Al Marjan Island under the chairmanship of HH Sheikh Saud bin Saqr Al Qasimi, with anchor partners that include Wynn Resorts, Hard Rock International, Anantara, and Marriott. The pipeline through 2030 spans hospitality, residential, retail, civic infrastructure, and the wider entertainment district aligned with RAK's 3.5 million visitor target.

The work is unusually consequential for an organisation of our size. A development manager here will sit across master-planning decisions that shape the coastline for the next generation. An asset manager will work directly with branded operators on a portfolio measured in billions. We are small enough that the work compounds, and senior enough that the seat at the table is real.

02 What we believe

Four ideas that shape the work.

These are not a poster on the wall — they are the lens we use when we hire, when we promote, and when we choose what to build next.

Value 01

Build for permanence.

We are building structures that should still be loved in fifty years. That means we choose the harder materials, we insist on the longer review, and we resist the brief that asks us to optimise for the photograph alone. Permanence is a discipline, not a style.

Value 02

Partner with conviction.

The names on our masterplan — Wynn, Hard Rock, Anantara, Marriott — are partners, not tenants. We commit early, we co-design, and we hold ourselves to operator-grade standards on infrastructure, permitting, and delivery. Partnership is how a master developer earns the second project.

Value 03

Stewardship of place.

Al Marjan Island is a coastline before it is a project. We are accountable to the marine environment, to the wider emirate, and to the people who will live, work, and visit here. Stewardship informs our sustainability brief, our coastal protection programme, and the height-restriction philosophy across the southern islets.

Value 04

Lead with curiosity.

We hire for taste and for restlessness. The work of a master developer changes — from sand and steel in 2015 to AI-assisted scheduling in 2026 — and the people who do it well are the ones who keep asking what comes next. Curiosity is what keeps a thirty-year project from becoming a forty-year one.

03 Benefits & rewards

Looked after, properly.

Our benefits are designed for the long career, not the short stint. They reflect what an organisation of Marjan's standing — government-linked, UAE-headquartered, and partner-grade — owes its people.

01 · Health

Comprehensive medical.

Top-tier international medical cover for you, your spouse, and your children — including dental, optical, mental health, and maternity. Pre-existing conditions accepted from day one. Annual executive health screening from year three.

02 · Time

Generous leave & sabbatical.

30 working days of annual leave, fully-paid sick leave to UAE standards, and a four-week paid sabbatical at every five-year anniversary. Parental leave for both parents — extended for senior leadership.

03 · Housing

Housing allowance.

Annual housing allowance benchmarked to RAK and Northern Dubai market rates, paid in advance. Relocation support for international hires, including temporary accommodation, school search assistance, and a settling-in stipend.

04 · Family

Education allowance.

Schooling allowance for up to three children, covering British, American, and IB curricula across the Northern Emirates. Annual return flights for the family to the country of origin. Visa sponsorship handled end-to-end.

05 · Wellbeing

Wellness & mobility.

Annual wellness allowance applicable to gym, racquet club, marine, or wellness memberships. Hotel and dining discount programme across Wynn, Hard Rock, Anantara, and Marriott partner properties. Salik & fuel benefit for the commute.

06 · Future

End-of-service & savings.

End-of-service gratuity in line with UAE labour law, supplemented by a voluntary savings scheme with employer match from year two. Long-term incentive plan for senior management aligned with multi-year project milestones.

04 Growth & development

The career, not just the role.

Our development framework is built around four progression pathways, each with structured learning, external mentorship, and a clear promotion ladder.

  • Technical specialists. Engineers, planners, designers, and project managers — supported with chartered accreditation, industry conferences, and a 60-hour annual CPD budget.
  • Commercial & investment. Asset managers, investment, capital markets — secondments into partner operators (Wynn, Hard Rock, Marriott), CFA & RICS sponsorship, and EY-led financial modelling academy.
  • Creative & brand. Marketing, communications, digital, design — annual creative residency programme, agency-side rotations, and a published portfolio review with our agency-of-record.
  • Leadership track. A 24-month executive programme run in partnership with INSEAD Middle East — open by nomination at director level and above.

Internal mobility is encouraged. More than a third of our 2025 senior promotions came from an internal move across functions.

We have a thirty-year masterplan and a small team. That combination is unusual, and it is the reason careers compound here in a way they do not elsewhere. — Abdulla Al Abdouli, CEO Marjan

07 Join us

Two ways to start.

Whether you have found your role or want us to keep you in mind, we read every application — and we reply.

  • Apply to an open role. Our current openings are kept current to the week. Each role lists the function lead, the location, and the expected level — and we commit to a first response within ten working days.
  • Join the talent network. Share a CV and the brief of work you are looking for. We will keep you in mind for upcoming openings and invite you to selected hiring events, including our annual Open Doors day on Al Marjan Island.
