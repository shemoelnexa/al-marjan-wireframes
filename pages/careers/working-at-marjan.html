02 Mandate

What We Do

Marjan is the master developer of Al Marjan Island — the government entity responsible for planning the districts, delivering the infrastructure, and stewarding the destination programmes that will define Ras Al Khaimah's position on the world stage.

Our mandate is broad in scope and long-horizon in pace. We sit across masterplanning and permitting, operator partnership management, branded-residence sales, investor relations, sustainability, and the civic infrastructure that makes Al Marjan Island function as a place. Every role connects directly to the construction of destinations that will serve the emirate well beyond any single project cycle.

03 Values

How We Work

Four principles underpin how decisions are made, how performance is evaluated, and how the organisation holds itself to account over a thirty-year horizon.

01

Ambition

02
