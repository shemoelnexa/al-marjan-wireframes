01 What We Do

What We Do

Marjan is the master developer of Al Marjan Island — the government-backed entity responsible for planning, infrastructure, and destination programming across Ras Al Khaimah's principal waterfront district. We work at the intersection of urban planning, hospitality, investment, and public policy to realise a thirty-year vision for the emirate.

Our work spans master-planning and permitting, operator partnership management, branded residence sales, investor relations, sustainability, and the civic infrastructure that makes Al Marjan Island function as a place. Every role at Marjan connects directly to the construction of destinations that will serve the emirate well beyond any single project cycle.

02 How We Work

How We Work

Ambition

Marjan operates against a masterplan horizon that extends to 2030 and beyond, with anchor partners that include Wynn Resorts, Hard Rock International, and Anantara. The scale of what we are delivering is disproportionate to our headcount, and that means every team member carries meaningful scope.

Discipline

A destination is only as good as the decisions made at the detail level — permitting timelines, brand-standard compliance, infrastructure sequencing, financial modelling rigour. We hold ourselves to the standards of the operators and investors we work with, not merely to the standards of the regional development market.

Ownership

Roles here are defined by outcomes, not job descriptions. A development manager will sit across decisions that shape the coastline for the next generation; an asset manager will work directly with global hotel operators on a portfolio measured in billions. We expect people to own their brief fully and raise their hand when the brief is wrong.

Stewardship

Al Marjan Island is a coastline before it is a project. We are accountable to the marine environment, to the residents of Ras Al Khaimah, and to the long-term health of the destination. Stewardship informs our sustainability programme, our coastal-protection work, and the height and density philosophy across the island.
