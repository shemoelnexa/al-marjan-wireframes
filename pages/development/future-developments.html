01 The Vision

A masterplan, measured in decades.

Al Marjan Island was conceived as a single, long-horizon real-estate programme — a four-islet archipelago reclaimed from the Arabian Gulf and developed in deliberate phases over twenty years.

Marjan serves as master developer on behalf of the Government of Ras Al Khaimah, sequencing the islands' infrastructure, hospitality, residential, and entertainment phases against a 2030 vision that places RAK among the top three tourism destinations in the Gulf. The pipeline you see today reflects 18 months of partner negotiations, AED 28 billion in committed CAPEX, and an underlying land bank that remains 41 percent unallocated.

The next phase is anchored by Wynn Al Marjan Island — opening 2027 as the UAE's first integrated resort — and a constellation of pipeline projects across the four islets calibrated to absorb the demand uplift that Wynn alone is forecast to generate.

05 Strategic Land Bank

Held back, by design.

Marjan's strategic land bank spans 3.2 million square metres across the four islets — 41 percent of total developable land, deliberately reserved for partner brands, capability sectors, and asset classes that will respond to demand patterns emerging from the 2027 anchor openings. The bank is segmented across four zones, each with distinct planning parameters, partner-fit criteria, and target hold horizons through 2035.

07 Co-invest

Three ways to enter the pipeline.

Marjan offers structured co-investment across announced anchors and forthcoming pipeline projects — calibrated for institutional capital, family offices, and operator-aligned investors.

  • Project-level equity. Direct SPV participation in confirmed pipeline hotels and residential schemes. Ticket size from AED 25 million, 8–12 year hold, target 14–18% IRR.
  • Branded residences. Title-deed ownership of Wynn, Hard Rock, Anantara, and JW Marriott-branded units — from AED 4.2 million, rental-pool integration, ~7% net yield.
  • Land-bank co-development. Side-by-side partnership with Marjan on next-phase parcels — operator-led, master-developer aligned, with structured exits at stabilisation.
