01 The Proposition

A home, kept like a suite.

Branded residences at Al Marjan Island are private homes operated to the standard of the adjacent hotel — by the hotel — under the same housekeeping, concierge, security, and food-and-beverage protocols.

The owner holds a freehold title deed registered with the Ras Al Khaimah Land Department. The operator runs the building, manages an optional rental pool when the owner is away, and underwrites the service standard that the brand is known for elsewhere in the world.

Globally, branded residences trade at a 15 – 30% premium to comparable non-branded stock and rent at materially higher occupancy. On Al Marjan Island, four operators have committed to delivering residential collections — the largest concentration of branded inventory anywhere on the Arabian Gulf.

04 Ownership Mechanics

Title, taxes, and the rental pool.

Ras Al Khaimah's freehold regime is among the most owner-friendly in the region — and the residences are structured to make hands-off ownership genuinely passive.

  • Freehold title. Registered with the RAK Land Department in the buyer's name (individual or corporate). Inheritance and resale rights unrestricted.
  • 100% foreign ownership. No nominee structures required. Title-deed issued upon completion of payment milestones.
  • Zero personal income tax. No tax on rental income for individual owners. Corporate owners taxed under the UAE 9% federal regime above AED 375,000 net profit.
  • Operator rental pool. Opt-in programme — owner sets blocked dates, operator handles bookings, housekeeping, and guest service. Revenue share typically 50/50 net of operating costs.
  • Service charges. AED 22 – 38 per sqft per year depending on operator, covering 24-hour concierge, security, building maintenance, and amenity access.
  • Residency visa. Property purchases above AED 2 million qualify the owner and immediate family for the 10-year UAE Golden Visa.
05 The Yield Case

A market about to compress.

08 Reserve

The path to title deed.

A typical reservation moves from expression of interest to keys in twelve to thirty months, tracking the construction calendar of each operator's tower.

  • Stage 1 · Expression. Submit the enquiry form. The Marjan residences desk circulates a confidential price list and floorplate options within 48 hours.
  • Stage 2 · Reservation. 10% deposit holds the unit. Sales & Purchase Agreement issued. RAK Land Department Oqood registration follows.
  • Stage 3 · Construction milestones. Staggered payments tied to substructure, structural completion, and façade. Typically five to six instalments.
  • Stage 4 · Handover. Snagging, furniture installation (if a branded furnished pack has been chosen), title-deed registration, and rental-pool enrolment.
