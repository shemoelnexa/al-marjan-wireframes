01Why Marjan

Why Operators Choose Marjan

As the master developer of Ras Al Khaimah, Marjan controls the land bank, the masterplan and the long-term investment pipeline that world-class operators need to make a new-market entry decision with confidence. Partnering with Marjan means partnering with the emirate's strategic growth agenda — backed by government, aligned with RAK Vision 2030 and future-ready in its infrastructure.

Land Bank & Masterplan Integration

Marjan holds a long-term land bank across Al Marjan Island, Al Wadi and RAK Central — all masterplanned to accommodate world-class hospitality at the scale global operators require. Each parcel is positioned within a planned placemaking context, not an ad hoc development site.

Capital Partners & Investor Pipeline

Marjan maintains an active investor pipeline across institutional capital, family offices and sovereign-adjacent funds, enabling deal structures from HMA and franchise through to full joint venture co-investment. Operators are not required to source their own capital partners to enter the platform.

Operational Backbone

Utilities, mobility networks, public realm and destination-level infrastructure are planned and delivered at the masterplan level — meaning operators arrive on a site that is connected, serviced and ready, rather than building the surrounding environment themselves. This materially reduces pre-opening complexity and timeline risk.

02Engagement Model

The Operator Engagement Model

01

Discovery & Fit

Marjan's hospitality team reviews the operator's brand profile, target segment and preferred operating model against the current parcel availability map. An initial site visit and masterplan briefing is arranged under NDA.

Weeks 1–4
02

Commercial Structuring

Term sheet negotiation covering operating model selection (HMA, franchise, lease or JV), fee envelope, design standards protocol and investment committee alignment on both sides. Marjan's legal and commercial team leads this phase.

Months 2–5
03

Design & Pre-Opening

Joint design governance begins — Marjan's development team works alongside the operator's technical services team to deliver a property that meets brand standards within the masterplan's design code and connectivity requirements. Pre-opening planning, hiring and systems integration run in parallel.

Years 1–3
04

Operations & Asset Stewardship

Post-opening, Marjan maintains an ongoing asset stewardship relationship — monitoring property performance against masterplan benchmarks, coordinating on public realm and destination programming, and providing access to the shared investment reporting framework available to all platform partners.

Open horizon
