01 The Invitation

A coastline still half-written.

Twenty-eight hospitality parcels span Al Marjan Island. Seventeen are committed to a confirmed operator. Eleven remain open — and we are now in active conversation with operators we have not yet announced.

Wynn Al Marjan Island opens Q1 2027. The Hard Rock Hotel follows in 2028. Rixos Bab Al Bahr, Hampton by Hilton, DoubleTree by Hilton, and Mövenpick are already operating successfully on and around the island. The next signatures will set the brand profile of the southern, central, and northern districts.

We are particularly interested in lifestyle and luxury operators not yet present in the UAE Northern Emirates — and in F&B and entertainment groups that can build a destination around their brand rather than a single outlet.

03 Operating Models

Four ways to partner.

Marjan is structure-agnostic. We work with each operator on the commercial form that best suits the brand strategy, the asset class, and the parcel.

M·01 Hotel Management Agreement
Model 01 · HMA

Operator runs, owner holds the asset

The Marjan-side owner SPV holds the asset and operating P&L. The operator runs day-to-day under a 20 – 25 year HMA. Standard base + incentive fees, area-of-protection clauses, technical-services agreement. Suits established global brands.

M·02 Franchise
Model 02 · Franchise

Brand licensed, operator-of-record on the island

Brand licence + plate, marketing system, central reservation access, and brand-standards governance. Day-to-day operation handled by a Marjan-approved operator on the island. Suits select-service and lifestyle brands scaling into the UAE.

M·03 Long-term lease
Model 03 · Lease

Operator takes the parcel, builds the asset

30 – 50 year ground lease at a base + variable rent. The operator finances, designs, constructs, and operates the asset under its own balance sheet. Suits operators that want full P&L ownership and design control.

M·04 Joint venture · co-investment
Model 04 · JV

Marjan and the operator on the same side

Joint SPV co-funded by Marjan and the operator (or its capital partner). Shared design, shared P&L, shared exit. The structure used for Wynn Al Marjan Island. Suits strategically important brands and integrated-resort projects.

Marjan is a master developer with a track record of moving fast on the commercial form. They will meet a brand on the structure that works, not impose one. — Operator advisory note, EY MENA Hospitality Outlook 2024
04 Who We Are Talking To

The brands we want next.

The current confirmed roster covers lifestyle, ultra-luxury entertainment, and select-service. The next signatures should fill three specific gaps.

  • Ultra-luxury wellness. A flagship wellness resort — adult-only, retreat-style, partnered with a credentialed medical-spa programme. 90 – 140 keys plus residences.
  • European luxury soft brands. Boutique collections within a major group — Edition, Hotel Indigo Collection, Curio, Autograph, MGallery, or independent brands such as Cheval Blanc, Aman, Rosewood, and Six Senses.
  • F&B and lifestyle entertainment. Beach club, day club, and standalone restaurant brands — Soho House, Nobu, Zuma, Cipriani, La Petite Maison, Cova, Sushi Samba, Coya, Em Sherif, Carbone, Catch — at venue or precinct scale.
  • Select-service expansion. Existing Hampton, Hilton Garden Inn, and Mövenpick performance has been strong. We are open to a second tranche of select-service product to support business travel and conference demand.
  • Branded residential operators. Beyond the four confirmed brands, we have parcels reserved for a fifth and sixth branded-residential collection.
05 Commercial Framework

The terms we work within.

Marjan structures hospitality deals around stabilised hotel IRRs of 12 – 16% for the asset owner, with operator economics aligned to industry-standard ranges. The numbers below represent the typical envelope for confirmed deals on the island as of 2025 — final terms are bespoke, negotiated parcel-by-parcel.

1 – 3% HMA base fee (% revenue)
8 – 12% Incentive fee (% GOP)
20 – 25yr Typical HMA term
12 – 16% Target owner IRR
