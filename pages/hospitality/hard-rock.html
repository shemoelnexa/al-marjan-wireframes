01 The Vision

Music, made coastal.

Hard Rock Al Marjan Island brings the brand's signature lifestyle — music, entertainment, design — to a master-planned destination 45 minutes north of Dubai.

The hotel anchors a wider entertainment district within Marjan's southern islet: a 1,500-capacity live music venue, signature culinary concepts curated with Hard Rock's global F&B team, a 1,400 sqm Rock Spa, and a beachfront pool deck running 220 metres along the Arabian Gulf.

It is Hard Rock's first integrated resort on the Arabian Gulf coast, and one of three lifestyle anchors confirmed for Al Marjan Island alongside Wynn Resorts and a forthcoming Anantara.

03 The Experience

Three pillars of Hard Rock.

The brand's seven-decade heritage in music and entertainment, expressed across accommodation, F&B, and wellness — all reframed for an Arabian Gulf coastline.

A·01 Accommodation
Pillar 01

Stay

600 keys ranging from deluxe rooms to signature suites — including a 320-square-metre Rock Star Suite with private pool, recording booth, and Stratocaster wall. Beach-villa cluster for extended stays. Branded-residence floors for ownership investors.

A·02 Entertainment & F&B
Pillar 02

Play

A 1,500-capacity live music venue programmed by Hard Rock's global music team. Five signature restaurants, a rooftop beach club running into the small hours, a Rock Shop concept store, and the iconic Cafe footprint on the public boardwalk.

A·03 Rock Spa & wellness
Pillar 03

Reset

A 1,400-square-metre Rock Spa with the brand's signature Rhythm & Motion treatments — bodywork choreographed to music. Outdoor wellness pavilions, a coastal hammam, and a beachfront yoga lawn open to dawn programming year-round.

Al Marjan Island is the natural home for our brand's first Gulf integrated resort — a coastline and an entertainment district that match our scale of ambition. — Hard Rock International, partnership statement, 2024

05 Economic Impact

The project's ripple effect.

Hard Rock contributes an estimated AED 3.1 billion in direct CAPEX and is forecast to generate $180–220 million in annual operating revenue at stabilisation. Combined with Wynn and the wider Marjan masterplan, the project lifts RAK's hospitality compression nights by an estimated 18–24 percentage points and supports the emirate's broader 2030 tourism objectives.

07 Invest

Three ways to co-invest.

Marjan offers structured exposure to the Hard Rock project across three vehicles, aligned with hospitality-grade hold periods and target IRR ranges.

  • Direct hotel asset. Equity in the hotel SPV — minimum ticket from AED 20 million, 7–10 year hold, 14–18% target IRR.
  • Branded residences. Title-deed ownership of Hard Rock-branded apartments and villas — from AED 4.5 million, rental-pool option, ~7% net yield.
  • Co-investment with Marjan. Side-by-side participation alongside the master developer on adjacent retail and F&B parcels.
