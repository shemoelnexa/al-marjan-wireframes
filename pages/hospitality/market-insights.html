01Macro Picture

The Macro Picture

RAK Coastline · F.01

Ras Al Khaimah is recording sustained growth across every major hospitality metric — visitor arrivals, average daily rate and occupancy are all tracking above regional peer destinations through 2025 and into 2026. The emirate's position as the second-largest hospitality market in the UAE by guest nights is now established, and the pipeline of 4,500+ new keys entering supply between 2025 and 2027 will accelerate that trajectory.

Visitor Growth

International visitor arrivals to Ras Al Khaimah grew 27% year-on-year in the most recent reporting period, outpacing both Dubai and Abu Dhabi on a percentage basis. The growth is broad-based across source markets, with the UK, Russia, India and the GCC all recording double-digit increases in overnight arrivals.

Average Daily Rate

The market ADR rose 18% over the same period, driven by the opening of new upper-upscale and luxury inventory and a structural shift in the visitor mix toward higher-spending leisure travellers. The ADR premium of Al Marjan Island properties over the broader RAK market has widened to approximately 22%.

Occupancy Outlook

Overall market occupancy is forecast to reach 83% at stabilisation following the opening of the Wynn Al Marjan Island integrated resort in 2027 — a level that would place RAK among the top five hospitality markets in the GCC by occupancy rate. Demand absorption of the incoming new supply is expected to be rapid given the structural keys gap relative to the RAK Vision 2030 visitor targets.

02Demand Composition

Where the Demand Is Coming From

42%International Leisure

International Leisure

The UK, Russia and Northern Europe collectively account for 42% of overnight demand, drawn by direct-flight connectivity, beachfront product and the emirate's positioning as a value-premium alternative to established Mediterranean and Indian Ocean resort destinations.

38%Regional Weekenders

Regional Weekenders

GCC short-break demand — primarily from KSA and Oman — accounts for 38% of total nights and is the fastest-growing segment by volume. The 35-minute drive from Dubai International Airport and the absence of a visa requirement for most GCC nationals are key demand catalysts.

20%MICE & Events

MICE & Events

Corporate, government and lifestyle events account for 20% of total demand and are growing at 31% per year — outpacing leisure on a percentage basis as the emirate's meetings infrastructure expands. The 145,000 sq ft meetings centre under development at Al Marjan Island will materially increase MICE capture from 2027.

Ras Al Khaimah's hospitality pipeline is unmatched in the GCC outside the established Dubai cluster, with new keys outpacing peer destinations through the end of the decade. Marjan Hospitality Research · 2026
