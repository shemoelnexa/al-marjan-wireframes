Hospitality · Market Insights

The Market Behind the Momentum

A hospitality market growing in scale and global appeal. Rising visitor numbers, expanding tourism infrastructure and long-term investment are creating one of the Middle East's most attractive hospitality markets.

Explore Investment Opportunities
01Introduction

Ras Al Khaimah's hospitality market is entering a new phase of growth. Hotels are welcoming more guests, generating higher revenues and delivering stronger operating performance, while the emirate continues to attract leading global hospitality brands. Landmark developments are further elevating its profile among travellers and investors.

This momentum is being underpinned by ambitious tourism targets, sustained infrastructure investment and new drivers of demand, creating the foundations for long-term industry growth.

For investors, developers and operators, it is a market defined not only by strong performance today, but by confidence in what comes next.

02Performance

A Market Delivering Results

The strongest hospitality markets combine rising demand with consistently strong operating performance. Ras Al Khaimah continues to deliver both.

In 2025, the emirate welcomed 1.36 million visitors and recorded 4.8 million guest nights. Growing demand was matched by stronger hotel revenues and improving operating performance, demonstrating the market's ability to convert tourism growth into commercial success.

The result was a record year for the hospitality sector. Hotel revenues reached new highs, investor confidence strengthened and the market demonstrated its capacity to absorb growing demand while creating long-term value. As new tourism infrastructure comes online, those fundamentals will only strengthen further.

A Clear Vision for Future Growth

Strong hospitality markets are shaped by long-term strategies that give investors, operators and developers confidence in what comes next.

Ras Al Khaimah's Tourism Vision 2030 provides that clarity. The strategy targets 3.5 million annual visitors by the end of the decade, almost tripling current visitation and positioning the emirate among the region's leading hospitality destinations.

Delivering this ambition will require continued investment across the tourism ecosystem, from hotels, resorts and branded residences to attractions, transport infrastructure and destination experiences. This growth is being pursued with a focus on sustainability, quality and long-term value creation.

For investors, the opportunity lies in the scale of development required to support the emirate's tourism ambitions. As demand expands, new opportunities are expected across hospitality, residential and mixed-use assets, supported by a clear government-backed roadmap for tourism-led growth.
