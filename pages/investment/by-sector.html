01Residential

Residential

Marjan's residential programme delivers branded, beachfront and mid-rise product to a market with one of the GCC's strongest rental yield profiles. Supply discipline maintained by the master developer ensures that new product enters the market in measured phases, protecting capital value over the hold horizon.

  • Asset class: Branded residential, beachfront, mid-rise.
  • Indicative hold horizon: 7–10 years.
  • Co-investment threshold: US$25M+.

02Hospitality

Hospitality

The hospitality sector is the primary catalyst for RAK's tourism economy and the anchor of Marjan's US$11 billion pipeline. Eight global operator partnerships across integrated resorts, branded hotels and nature-led retreats provide investors with operator-backed yield and world-class asset management discipline across a diversified programme.

  • Asset class: Integrated resort, branded hotel, boutique retreat.
  • Indicative hold horizon: 10–15 years.
  • Co-investment threshold: US$50M+.

03Commercial

Commercial

Commercial product across RAK Central and the Marjan beach district positions investors ahead of the emirate's corporate relocation and lifestyle retail wave. Office, retail and mixed-use assets are structured as long-term income instruments with phased delivery aligned to occupier demand and the broader masterplan programme.

  • Asset class: Grade-A office, lifestyle retail, mixed-use.
  • Indicative hold horizon: 8–12 years.
  • Co-investment threshold: US$20M+.

04Staff Accommodation

Staff Accommodation

As Ras Al Khaimah's workforce grows in step with its hospitality pipeline, purpose-built staff accommodation emerges as a discrete, yield-oriented asset class with structural operator demand. Marjan's programme delivers compliant, well-amenitised villages adjacent to the emirate's major employment anchors, providing investors with income-generating infrastructure at scale.

  • Asset class: Purpose-built workforce housing, operator villages.
  • Indicative hold horizon: 5–8 years.
  • Co-investment threshold: US$10M+.
