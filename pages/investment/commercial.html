01 The Thesis

The economy behind the tourism economy.

Hospitality is the visible part of RAK's 2030 plan. The commercial real-estate response to it is the under-priced part.

The 14+ operators committing to the island need regional headquarters, sales offices, and back-of-house corporate space. The 3.5 million projected overnight visitors need destination retail. The professional-services ecosystem — legal, audit, advisory, design, F&B operators — is migrating up the E311 corridor from Dubai.

Colliers' Office & Retail Supply Note 2024 sizes the gap at 41,000 sqm of Grade A office and 90,000 sqm of retail GLA by 2030. The current pipeline addresses approximately 45% of office demand and 55% of retail demand.

03 Sub-Segments

Four discrete demand pools.

The commercial gap is not monolithic. Each pool has its own anchor tenant profile, rent comparables, and lease structure — four distinct ways to underwrite the same 131,000 sqm opportunity.

A·01 Grade A office HQ
Pool 01 · 23,000 sqm

Grade A operator HQ

Regional headquarters for the 14+ hospitality and F&B operators committing to the island. Build-to-suit and pre-let forward funding both available. Indicative rents AED 1,250–1,450 per sqm p.a., 7–10 year leases with indexation, blue-chip covenants.

A·02 Family office & professional services
Pool 02 · 18,000 sqm

Family office & pro services

Smaller-floor-plate office for family offices migrating from Dubai, legal and audit firms following hospitality clients, and design and advisory practices serving the master-plan build-out. AED 1,050–1,250 per sqm, 5–7 year leases.

A·03 Destination retail
Pool 03 · 60,000 sqm

Destination retail

Anchor luxury retail and lifestyle precincts adjacent to the Wynn and Hard Rock entertainment districts. Mix of flagship brands, food halls, and curated lifestyle tenants. Base rent + turnover, average passing rent AED 4,200 per sqm p.a.

A·04 Convenience & F&B
Pool 04 · 30,000 sqm

Convenience & F&B

Resident-serving convenience retail, supermarkets, neighbourhood F&B, fitness, and wellness operators across the residential precincts. Long, indexed leases, stable tenant mix, AED 2,400–3,200 per sqm.

Al Marjan Island's commercial pipeline is the only forward-funded coastal Grade A market in the Northern Emirates with a dated demand catalyst against operator tenant covenants. — Colliers Office & Retail Supply Note, 2024

05 Rental Comparables

Priced against the Northern Emirates corridor.

Al Marjan Island commercial rents price at a 22–28% discount to Dubai Marina Grade A office and a 12–18% premium to RAK mainland comparables. Retail rents price competitively against Mall of the Emirates inline tenants, with a structurally higher tourist-spend mix. Vacancy across the pipeline is forecast to stabilise below 6% by 2028.
