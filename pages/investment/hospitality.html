01Introduction

Tourism Growth Creates Opportunity. Hospitality Supply Captures It.

Ras Al Khaimah is entering a period in which visitor demand, destination investment and global visibility are expanding simultaneously. Hotels are welcoming more guests, generating stronger revenues and benefiting from rising occupancy and room rates as landmark developments continue to elevate the emirate's international profile.

Ambitious tourism targets and evolving visitor expectations are creating demand for new hotels, resorts, branded residences and integrated lifestyle developments. For investors, the opportunity lies in delivering the next generation of hospitality assets within a market where demand continues to outpace supply.

02The Market

A Market Ready for New Supply

Ras Al Khaimah's hospitality sector has entered a new phase of growth. The challenge is no longer attracting visitors but accommodating them.

Tourism Vision 2030 targets 3.5 million annual visitors by the end of the decade. To meet the rise in demand, Colliers estimates the emirate will require approximately 6,000 additional hotel keys.

Strong market fundamentals reinforce that opportunity. Rising occupancy, room rates and hotel revenues demonstrate the sector's capacity to absorb new supply while maintaining healthy operating performance.

For investors, the opportunity is clear: deliver the hospitality assets needed to support the emirate's next phase of tourism growth.

04The Wynn Effect
Wynn Al Marjan Island · F.05

The Wynn Effect

The UAE's first integrated resort, the US$5.1 billion mega-project is elevating Ras Al Khaimah's global profile, attracting international travellers, hospitality brands and investment.

EY estimates that Wynn, combined with Ras Al Khaimah's wider destination development, could help increase annual visitation to between 3.5 and 4 million by 2030, supported by significant growth in both overnight and day visitors.

For investors, Wynn's significance extends beyond the resort itself. It acts as a catalyst for broader tourism investments, creating opportunities spanning hotels, resorts, branded residences, retail, leisure and mixed-use developments.

05Performance

A Sector Already Delivering on its Promise

Future demand matters, but Ras Al Khaimah's hospitality sector is already demonstrating the strength of its market fundamentals.

According to RAKTDA's latest Market Insights report, visitor arrivals reached 1.36 million in 2025, with guest nights increasing to 4.8 million. Rising demand translated into stronger performance across every key hotel metric, including occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and hotel revenues.

For investors, these results demonstrate a market that is already converting tourism growth into commercial performance while retaining significant capacity for future expansion.
