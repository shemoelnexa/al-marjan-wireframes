01 The Thesis

The Wynn effect, priced in.

Wynn Al Marjan Island opens Q1 2027. $3.9 billion of CAPEX, 1,542 keys, the UAE's first integrated resort. It will reset RAK's demand curve.

EY's MENA Hospitality Outlook 2024 sizes the supply response at 6,000 incremental keys needed by 2030 to absorb forecast demand — even on a conservative arrivals projection. The existing pipeline addresses approximately 60% of that gap.

Marjan operates as the single counterparty for hospitality investors sizing into the gap. We hold parcels with the right beachfront orientation, the right adjacencies to Wynn and Hard Rock, and the operator relationships to deliver a 36–42 month opening cycle from term sheet.

03 Three Routes

Direct, JV, or fund.

Three structured ways into the gap, sized for different investor profiles — from family offices co-investing alongside Marjan to operators developing flagship integrated resorts.

A·01 Direct hotel asset
Route 01 · Highest control

Direct asset

100% equity ownership of a hotel SPV holding the land lease and operator HMA. Marjan provides the parcel, infrastructure, permitting alignment and operator introductions. Typical ticket AED 400M–1.5B, target IRR 16–18%, 7–10 year hold.

A·02 JV with Marjan
Route 02 · Balanced

Joint venture

30–50% equity alongside Marjan in the hotel SPV. Shared CAPEX exposure, shared upside, governance via JV board. Typical ticket AED 100–500M, target IRR 14–17%, 7–10 year hold. The most common route for family offices and value-add funds.

A·03 Hospitality fund
Route 03 · Diversified

Marjan Hospitality Fund

LP participation in a diversified Marjan-curated portfolio of four to six hotel assets across the island. Min ticket AED 25M, target net IRR 14–16%, eight-year fund life with optional extensions. Quarterly NAV reporting.

Comparable jurisdictions saw +30–40 percentage-point luxury occupancy lifts in the three years following their first integrated-resort opening. RAK is dated to follow the same demand curve, with a materially shorter supply response. — EY MENA Hospitality Outlook, 2024

05 Demand Modelling

RevPAR, ADR, and the curve.

EY underwriting models RAK luxury ADR at AED 1,650 stabilised (versus AED 1,180 in 2023) and stabilised occupancy at 78–82% for well-located beachfront product. Hospitality compression nights — defined as system-wide 95%+ occupancy — are forecast to rise from 38 nights in 2023 to 110+ post-2027, supporting both rate and rate confidence.
