The Hospitality Case

Ras Al Khaimah's tourism economy is entering its most transformative decade, underpinned by the Wynn Al Marjan Island opening, a pipeline of world-class branded product and a government commitment to reaching 3.5 million visitors annually by 2030. Marjan's hospitality investment programme offers structured access to this growth story — through direct hotel equity, operator joint ventures and branded residence co-investment — with the certainty of a master developer managing land, infrastructure and planning on behalf of all partners.

Visitor growth

RAK's total visitor arrivals have grown at a compound rate of 17% over the past three years, driven by increased airlift, the UAE's global connectivity and a growing international awareness of the emirate as a leisure benchmark. The 2027 Wynn opening is forecast to create a step-change in this trajectory, drawing a new category of high-spending international visitor.

Operator pipeline

Marjan's eight confirmed operator partnerships span the full spectrum of hospitality product — from ultra-luxury integrated resort to nature-led boutique — providing investors with a diversified portfolio of operator-backed assets at varying price points and risk profiles. Each partnership is structured with Marjan as master developer, ensuring land-use alignment, planning certainty and long-term stewardship.

Land bank availability

With 41% of developable land within the Marjan masterplan still unallocated, the hospitality land bank represents a horizon of investment opportunity extending well beyond the 2032 delivery window. Future-ready parcels are held in reserve against demand patterns emerging from the current cycle of anchor openings.
