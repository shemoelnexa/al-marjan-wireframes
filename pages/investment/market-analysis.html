01Introduction

Market performance tells one story. Independent analysis helps explain where the market is heading next.

Leading advisory firms including Colliers, JLL and Savills have examined the forces shaping Ras Al Khaimah's growth, from tourism and real estate to infrastructure, business activity and investment.

While each approaches the market from a different perspective, their conclusions point in the same direction: Ras Al Khaimah is entering a sustained period of growth, underpinned by economic diversification, strategic investment and a rising global profile.

02Colliers

Growth Built on Strong Foundations

Core finding: growth is being driven by long-term economic diversification rather than a single catalyst.

Colliers' Ras Al Khaimah: Real Estate Transformation 2025/2026 identifies economic diversification, infrastructure investment and long-term planning as the foundations of the emirate's growth. Rather than being driven by a single catalyst, its success reflects nearly two decades of sustained investment across manufacturing, trade, logistics, tourism and business.

The report highlights the strength of Ras Al Khaimah's diversified economy, with more than 40% of GDP generated by manufacturing, trade and logistics. This broad economic base is supporting population growth, employment and long-term demand across the residential, hospitality and commercial real estate sectors.

Colliers also points to the growing role of RAKEZ in strengthening the emirate's business ecosystem. More than 8,500 companies were registered during the first half of 2025, an increase of 43% year-on-year that reinforces Ras Al Khaimah's appeal as a destination for business and investment.

Investment implication — A diversified economy, expanding business base and growing population provide a strong foundation for long-term real estate and infrastructure investment.

03Savills
Branded residences, Al Marjan Island · F.02

Real Estate Demand is Outpacing Supply

Core finding: population growth and investor interest are creating sustained demand for new residential supply.

Savills identifies Ras Al Khaimah as one of the UAE's fastest-evolving real estate markets, driven by population growth, tourism expansion and rising international investor demand.

The analyst highlights growing interest in waterfront communities, branded residences and lifestyle-led developments, particularly across Al Marjan Island and the emirate's expanding coastal corridor. As demand continues to accelerate, Savills identifies a widening gap between future housing needs and available supply.

For investors, this supply-demand imbalance is creating opportunities across villas, apartments, branded residences and mixed-use communities as the market responds to the emirate's next phase of growth.

Investment implication — A widening gap between future housing demand and available supply is creating opportunities across villas, apartments, branded residences and mixed-use communities.

04Colliers
Wynn Al Marjan Island · F.04

Beyond the Wynn Effect

The announcement of Wynn Al Marjan Island marked a defining moment in Ras Al Khaimah's evolution. But according to Colliers, the emirate's transformation was already well underway.

The analyst identifies the mega project as an accelerator rather than the catalyst for growth. Years of infrastructure investment, economic diversification and destination development had already created the foundations for large-scale investment and international recognition.

Its significance therefore extends beyond the resort itself. Wynn Al Marjan Island has strengthened confidence in Ras Al Khaimah's investment environment, attracted international developers and accelerated the emergence of a luxury coastal corridor spanning Al Marjan Island, Mina Al Arab and Al Hamra.

Investment implication — The strongest investment markets are built on multiple growth drivers. Colliers identifies Wynn as an accelerator of an already established growth story rather than its sole catalyst.
Evidence & data points

  • Supporting the growth of a new luxury coastal corridor

05JLL

A Market Gaining Global Recognition

Core finding: Ras Al Khaimah is demonstrating many of the characteristics shared by established global resort destinations.

JLL identifies many of the characteristics Ras Al Khaimah shares with established global resort destinations that consistently attract strong interest from investors, operators and developers.

Its RAK Hotel Investment Study highlights the emirate's strong operating performance, supportive investment environment and long-term tourism outlook. The report also identifies many of the characteristics shared by successful international leisure destinations, including strong connectivity, government support, infrastructure investment and a diversified tourism offering.

For investors, it signals a market whose underlying fundamentals increasingly align with those of the world's leading resort destinations.

Investment implication — As Ras Al Khaimah's market fundamentals continue to align with those of established international resort destinations, its appeal to global investors will only strengthen.

06Consensus

What the Experts Agree On

Each advisor examines Ras Al Khaimah through a different lens, yet they reach a remarkably consistent conclusion.

Colliers highlights the strength of the emirate's economic foundations. Savills identifies growing demand across the residential market. JLL points to a hospitality sector increasingly comparable with established global resort destinations.

Taken together, these independent perspectives present a compelling picture: Ras Al Khaimah is entering a new phase of growth underpinned by economic diversification, sustained investment and expanding opportunities across hospitality, residential and commercial real estate.

For investors, the message is clear. The emirate's long-term growth story is supported not by a single report or development, but by a broad consensus among leading independent advisors.
