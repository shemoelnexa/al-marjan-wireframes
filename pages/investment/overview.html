The Investment Thesis

Marjan's masterplanned programme represents a once-in-a-generation alignment of government mandate, land bank scale and operator-driven demand. The emirate's trajectory under RAK Vision 2030 creates a structural demand floor that distinguishes Ras Al Khaimah from speculative emerging markets. Investors entering now access a future-ready destination at an inflection point, with a disciplined master developer managing the sequencing and stewardship of every phase.

Economic alignment with RAK Vision 2030

Every investment opportunity within the Marjan portfolio is calibrated against the emirate's long-term economic strategy, ensuring capital is deployed into sectors with structural government support. This alignment translates directly into planning certainty, infrastructure commitment and policy continuity across the investment horizon.

Diversified sector exposure

The portfolio spans residential, hospitality, commercial and staff accommodation — four distinct asset classes with complementary risk-return profiles and differentiated capital structures. Investors may take a single-sector position or participate across the platform with a co-investment mandate.

Long-term hold horizon

Marjan structures investments for a 7–10 year hold horizon, aligned with the delivery and stabilisation phases of each programme. This disciplined approach ensures that capital is committed to assets that have reached their full performance potential before exits are considered.
