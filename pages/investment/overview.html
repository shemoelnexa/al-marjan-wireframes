01 The Thesis

One island, four sectors, one cycle.

Al Marjan Island is in the build-out phase of a once-in-a-generation coastal masterplan — the kind of cycle real-estate capital has only seen a handful of times in the GCC.

The catalyst is real and dated. Wynn Al Marjan Island — a $3.9 billion, 1,542-key integrated resort — opens Q1 2027, the first of its kind on the Arabian Gulf. Ras Al Khaimah's tourism master plan targets 3.5 million overnight visitors by 2030, more than double 2023 arrivals.

The supply response is materially short. Knight Frank, Colliers and EY each independently identify shortfalls of 45,000 residential units, 6,000 hotel keys, 131,000 sqm of commercial space and 7,200 staff accommodation units across the emirate by 2030 — all of it concentrated within or adjacent to the Marjan masterplan.

Marjan, as the government-mandated master developer, controls the parcel pipeline and the JV cadence. We are the single regulatory, commercial and infrastructure counterparty for any investor sizing into the cycle.

03 Jurisdiction

Why Ras Al Khaimah, and why now.

A sovereign jurisdiction rated A by both Fitch and S&P, with one of the most investor-friendly real-estate frameworks in the GCC — and a master developer empowered to transact at institutional speed.

A·01 Sovereign & tax framework
Pillar 01

Capital security

A-band sovereign credit. Corporate tax capped at 9% above the AED 375,000 threshold, 0% below. No personal income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding on dividend or interest repatriation. UAE has a 140+ country double-tax treaty network.

A·02 Ownership & residency
Pillar 02

Ownership rights

100% foreign freehold across the island. Title deeds issued by the RAK Lands Department. Property investments above AED 2 million qualify for the UAE's 10-year Golden Visa, with family sponsorship and no minimum stay requirement.

A·03 Master developer access
Pillar 03

Single counterparty

Marjan is the government-mandated master developer with control of the parcel pipeline, infrastructure scheduling and JV cadence. One counterparty for land, permitting alignment, utilities programming and the wider masterplan.

Ras Al Khaimah combines an A-rated sovereign, a clear tourism strategy with a dated catalyst, and a master developer that can transact at institutional speed. That combination is rare in the GCC. — Marjan investment thesis, 2024

05 The Demand Curve

The Wynn effect — and what comes after.

Comparable jurisdictions — Macau in 2007, Singapore in 2010 — saw luxury-segment occupancy rise 30–40 percentage points and visitor spend climb $1.5–2.5 billion in the three years following the first integrated-resort opening. RAK is dated to follow the same curve, with Wynn anchoring a hospitality district that will include Hard Rock, Anantara, Nikki Beach, JW Marriott and a confirmed pipeline of more than 14 branded operators by 2028.
