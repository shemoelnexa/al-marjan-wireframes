Deep dive

The eight steps.

Each stage has a defined deliverable, owner on both sides, and a typical window. Timelines compress for repeat counterparties and extend for first-time UAE entrants.

01

Initial enquiry

You complete the enquiry form or write directly to invest@marjan.ae. Our investor-relations team responds within five working days with a brief acknowledgement, an indicative fit assessment, and a calendar link for a discovery call.

No NDA is required at this stage — only a one-page mandate brief covering ticket size, sector preference, hold horizon, and decision-maker contact.

Week 1
02

Discovery call & shortlist

A 45-minute video call with Marjan's investment team — typically the Head of Investment and the relevant sector lead (hospitality, residential, mixed-use, or infrastructure). We walk you through the live pipeline and tag three to five opportunities matched to your mandate.

You leave the call with a written shortlist, an indicative term-sheet template, and a draft NDA for execution.

Weeks 2–3
03

Information memorandum & data room

On NDA execution, you receive a 60- to 90-page Confidential Information Memorandum covering project economics, planning status, design package, construction timeline, and Marjan's contribution to the SPV.

Concurrently, you are credentialed into a virtual data room hosted on Intralinks — typically organised across eight folders: corporate, planning, technical, financial, commercial, legal, ESG, and management.

Week 4
04

Site visit

A guided two-day visit to Al Marjan Island. Day one covers the master plan, the relevant site parcel, and on-site meetings with project leadership; day two pairs you with operating partners (Wynn, Hard Rock, Nikki Beach, or others as relevant) for asset-level conversation.

Marjan covers ground arrangements; you cover flights and accommodation — Hilton Garden Inn, DoubleTree, or the upcoming Hard Rock once open.

Week 5
05

Term sheet

A non-binding term sheet is exchanged covering ticket size, equity / debt split, governance rights, drag and tag, distribution waterfall, exit mechanics, and conditions precedent.

Three to four rounds of mark-up are typical. The document is not legally binding except for exclusivity, confidentiality, and expenses — but it sets the commercial perimeter for everything that follows.

Weeks 6–8
06

Due diligence

Legal, commercial, and technical DD run in parallel. Legal is typically led by Clifford Chance, A&O Shearman, or Al Tamimi on the investor side; commercial by EY, KPMG, or PwC; technical by Mott MacDonald, Arup, or AECOM.

ESG due diligence has become standard since 2024 — typically a four-week workstream covering EHS, social, and governance dimensions against IFC Performance Standards.

Weeks 9–16
07

Documentation

SPA, shareholders' agreement, and any ancillary instruments (loan notes, put / call options, brand-licence side letters) are negotiated and finalised. Local counsel registers the SPV with RAKEZ or RAK ICC depending on structure.

Conditions precedent typically include corporate authorisations, regulatory approvals, third-party consents, and confirmation of source-of-funds satisfactory to UAE compliance standards.

Weeks 17–22
08

Closing

Conditions precedent satisfied, funds flow to escrow and release to the SPV against signed completion certificates. Share registry updated, board appointments made, and the first quarterly investor report is scheduled within 90 days.

Marjan's asset-management team takes over relationship continuity from investor relations from this point forward.

Before you enquire

What to prepare.

Three documents materially compress the front half of the process. None are mandatory at first contact — but every week we save here is a week added to diligence.

Document 01

Mandate brief

A one-page summary of your investment mandate: ticket size range, sector preference (hospitality / residential / mixed-use / infrastructure), geographic exposure already held, target hold period, and yield versus capital-gain split.

Most family offices and sovereign wealth funds already have this in board-approved form. If you don't, our team can share a template — a two-paragraph version is enough for the first conversation.

Download template (PDF) →
Document 02

Capital structure

An outline of how the investment will sit inside your wider structure — investing entity, jurisdiction of incorporation, beneficial-ownership chain, and whether the ticket will be held directly or through a feeder vehicle.

This accelerates KYC, anti-bribery clearance, and the SPA negotiation. UAE-resident investors typically use a RAK ICC or DIFC Foundation; international investors most commonly route through a Cayman or Luxembourg SPV.

Structuring checklist (PDF) →
Document 03

Operating partners (if relevant)

For investors bringing their own operator, brand, or development partner to a co-invest opportunity, a written outline of the proposed partnership terms speeds matching against Marjan's existing operating-partner roster.

Most hospitality co-invests sit alongside an existing Marjan partner (Wynn, Hard Rock, Anantara, Nikki Beach). Greenfield brand introductions are welcome but extend the timeline by four to six weeks.
