The Four Stages

01

Discovery & Mandate Fit

An initial confidential conversation between the prospective investor and Marjan's investor relations team establishes mandate parameters, sector preference and capital structure. The outcome is a written mandate-fit assessment confirming which programmes within the portfolio are appropriate for further engagement.

Weeks 1–2
02

Diligence & Structuring

Marjan provides a structured information package — sector brief, financial model, legal framework and programme timeline — under a signed non-disclosure agreement. The investor conducts independent diligence with their advisers while Marjan's finance and legal teams respond to queries and refine the capital structure in parallel.

Weeks 3–8
03

Commitment & Documentation

On completion of diligence, a term sheet is agreed and legal documentation — SPV formation, subscription agreements and governance frameworks — is prepared by both parties' legal teams. Marjan's internal governance process runs concurrently, ensuring that board approval and counterparty confirmation are completed within the documented timeline.

Weeks 9–14
04

Deployment & Handover

Capital is deployed into the agreed programme on the terms set out in the documentation, with Marjan providing quarterly investor reporting from the first full quarter following close. The handover phase — including title transfer, key delivery and rental-pool registration for residential products — is managed by Marjan's asset management team to an agreed programme-specific schedule.

Quarter following close
Bespoke structures: For institutional partners with bespoke structuring needs, Marjan's investor relations team works alongside legal, finance and operating teams to tailor the engagement model. Off-plan structures, phased capital deployment and joint venture frameworks are each available subject to programme suitability and mandate alignment.
02Documentation

What You'll Need

The following documentation is required from all investors prior to commitment. Marjan's investor relations team provides a full checklist and document templates on request at Stage 2.

  • KYC documentation — passport, proof of address, corporate registration where applicable.
  • Source of funds verification — bank statements, audited accounts or a solicitor's letter confirming fund origin.
  • Authorised signatory letter — confirming the individual(s) authorised to sign on behalf of the investing entity.
  • Tax-residency declaration — confirming the investor's jurisdiction of tax residence for reporting purposes.
  • Bank reference letter — a formal reference from the investor's primary banking relationship, issued within 90 days.
