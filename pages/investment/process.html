01Prologue

Marjan's investment process is disciplined, predictable, and structured around long-term partnership — designed to give institutional capital the confidence to commit and the clarity to deploy.

Every engagement follows the same four-stage architecture: an initial mandate-fit conversation, a structured diligence and structuring phase, formal commitment and documentation, and finally capital deployment with ongoing asset stewardship. The sequence is consistent across all capital partners and product types; the conversation within each stage is calibrated to each investor's specific mandate, jurisdiction and structure.

03Detail

Inside each stage

Each stage has a defined set of participants, deliverables and decision points. The sequence below describes what happens, who is involved and what you will see and sign at each step.

01

Discovery & Mandate Fit

An initial confidential call is arranged between the prospective investor — typically a CIO, head of alternatives or family office principal — and Marjan's investor relations team. The conversation establishes mandate parameters: target sector, preferred capital structure (equity, mezzanine or debt), minimum and maximum ticket size, hold period and jurisdiction requirements. Marjan provides a one-page programme overview under a light confidentiality agreement ahead of the call.

Within five business days of the call, Marjan issues a written mandate-fit assessment. This two-page document confirms which programmes within the portfolio are appropriate for further engagement, outlines any structural constraints, and proposes a diligence timeline. If no programme fits the mandate, Marjan will say so clearly and may suggest a return window for future pipeline.

Weeks 1–2 · Mandate-fit assessment issued within 5 business days of initial call
02

Diligence & Structuring

On execution of a mutual non-disclosure agreement, the investor receives access to Marjan's structured data room. The data room contains the programme information memorandum, audited project financials, legal title confirmation, masterplan documents, sector analysis prepared by an independent adviser, and the proposed capital structure term sheet. Marjan's finance and legal teams are available for weekly Q&A calls throughout this phase, and a dedicated investor relations contact is assigned for the duration.

In parallel, Marjan's structuring team works with the investor's advisers to finalise the appropriate vehicle — whether a direct SPV, a co-investment alongside an existing fund, or a jointly managed entity. Entity jurisdiction, tax treatment and reporting obligations are documented in a structuring memorandum issued by week six. The investor is expected to have completed independent legal and financial diligence by the end of week eight, with a written diligence completion notice submitted to Marjan's investor relations team.

Weeks 3–8 · Data room open from Week 3 · Structuring memorandum by Week 6 · Diligence completion notice by Week 8
03

Commitment & Documentation

On receipt of the diligence completion notice, Marjan issues a binding term sheet within three business days. The term sheet covers investment amount, capital structure, governance rights, return profile, exit mechanics and reporting obligations. Both parties' legal teams then prepare definitive documentation: a subscription agreement, shareholders' agreement (where applicable), SPV formation documents and any required regulatory filings. Marjan's internal governance process — board approval and investment committee sign-off — runs in parallel to avoid extending the overall timeline.

KYC and source-of-funds clearance is managed by Marjan's compliance team, who issue a documentation checklist at the start of Stage 3. Required documents include passport copies, corporate registration extracts, an authorised signatory letter, a tax-residency declaration and a bank reference letter. All KYC materials are submitted via a secure portal and reviewed within five business days. Signing of definitive documents occurs once compliance clearance is confirmed, typically in week thirteen or fourteen.

Weeks 9–14 · Term sheet issued within 3 days of diligence notice · Definitive docs signed by Week 14
04

Deployment & Handover

Capital is deployed into the agreed programme in accordance with the drawdown schedule set out in the subscription agreement. Marjan's treasury team issues a drawdown notice at least ten business days before each tranche, confirming the amount, receiving account details and the deployment milestone it funds. The investor receives a deployment confirmation within two business days of each transfer, signed by Marjan's CFO.

From the first full quarter following close, the investor receives a quarterly report covering programme progress, financial performance against plan, NAV update and any material events. For residential products, the handover phase — including title transfer, key delivery and rental-pool registration — is managed by Marjan's asset management team to a programme-specific schedule agreed at the time of signing. A dedicated asset manager is assigned to each capital partner for the life of the investment.

Post-close · Drawdown notice 10 days prior to each tranche · Quarterly reporting from first full quarter
