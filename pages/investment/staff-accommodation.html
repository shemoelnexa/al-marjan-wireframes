The Staff Accommodation Case

The rapid expansion of Marjan's hospitality pipeline creates a structural, operator-driven demand for purpose-built workforce housing that the existing RAK market cannot absorb. This imbalance presents investors with a high-yield, low-correlation opportunity — income-generating infrastructure that is insulated from cyclical residential market movements and underpinned by long-term lease commitments from the emirate's most creditworthy hospitality operators. Marjan's stewardship of the masterplan ensures that accommodation is positioned adjacent to employment anchors, reducing operator cost and ensuring strong occupancy from day one.

Structural workforce demand

The emirate's hospitality pipeline will require more than 20,000 additional workers by 2030 — a scale of demand that cannot be met by informal or fragmented accommodation solutions. Marjan's programme delivers compliant, scalable villages that allow operators to manage their workforce housing commitments as a single institutional relationship rather than a dispersed, unmanaged cost.

Operator long-term commitment

Marjan's eight confirmed hospitality partners have each entered into long-term workforce housing discussions as a condition of their development agreements, providing investors with a pipeline of creditworthy, multi-year lease counterparties. This operator-anchored structure creates a yield profile that is closer to social infrastructure than speculative residential development.

Regulatory and compliance alignment

All Marjan staff accommodation product is developed to UAE Ministry of Human Resources standards, ensuring full regulatory compliance and eliminating the enforcement risk that affects informal market alternatives. This compliance positioning makes the product attractive to global operators managing ESG obligations across their development pipelines.
