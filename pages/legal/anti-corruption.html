Anti-Corruption Policy

Marjan's commitment to operating with integrity, in line with UAE federal law and international anti-corruption standards.

Effective Date01 January 2026
Last Reviewed01 January 2026
Policy OwnerGeneral Counsel
Document RefMJN-POL-AC-001
Article 01

Purpose & Scope

This policy sets out Marjan's expectations regarding the prevention of bribery and corruption. It applies to all directors, officers, employees, contractors and agents of Marjan LLC and its subsidiaries.

Article 02

Prohibited Conduct

Marjan prohibits all forms of bribery and corruption, including offering, requesting or accepting any benefit intended to influence a business or government decision.

Article 03

Gifts & Hospitality

Gifts and hospitality must be modest in value, transparent and consistent with normal business courtesy. All gifts above the threshold defined in Marjan's internal guidance must be approved in advance.

Article 04

Facilitation Payments

Facilitation payments are prohibited. No employee or agent of Marjan may make small payments to expedite routine government processes.

Article 05

Third-Party Diligence

Marjan conducts risk-based due diligence on counterparties, agents and intermediaries. Higher-risk relationships require enhanced review and approval before contracting.

Article 06

Reporting & Whistleblowing

Marjan operates a confidential reporting channel for concerns relating to bribery, corruption or related misconduct. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.

Article 07

Sanctions for Breach

Breach of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment or engagement, and may be reported to relevant authorities where required by law.
