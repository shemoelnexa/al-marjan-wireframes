Data protection

How Marjan collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data — and how you can exercise your rights under UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021.

Effective date 15 March 2026
Last reviewed 1 May 2026
Version v3.4
Jurisdiction Ras Al Khaimah, UAE
Article 01

Controller identification

For the purposes of UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the "UAE PDPL") and the regulations issued under it, Marjan (Master Developer Real Estate L.L.C.) is the data controller of the personal data described in this notice.

The registered office of the controller is at the RAK Government Complex, Al Nakheel, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. Marjan has appointed a Data Protection Officer who is responsible for overseeing compliance with this notice and for liaising with the UAE Data Office on supervisory matters.

Data Protection Officer

The Data Protection Officer may be contacted by email at dpo@marjan.example, or by post to the Data Protection Officer, Marjan (Master Developer Real Estate L.L.C.), RAK Government Complex, Al Nakheel, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. Correspondence may be in English or in Arabic.

Marjan collects personal data directly from you, from your authorised representatives, and from a limited set of third parties such as appointed brokers, recruitment partners, and identity-verification providers. Article 03

Lawful bases

Marjan processes personal data only where the processing has a lawful basis under Article 4 of the UAE PDPL. The principal bases on which Marjan relies are set out below.

  • Consent. You have given your consent to the processing of your personal data for one or more specific purposes, for example to receive marketing communications about future launches.
  • Contract. The processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which you are a party, for example a reservation agreement, a sale and purchase agreement, or an employment contract.
  • Legitimate interests. The processing is necessary for the legitimate interests pursued by Marjan or by a third party, where those interests are not overridden by your interests or fundamental rights. Legitimate interests include securing the Website, preventing fraud, and developing our products and services.
  • Legal obligation. The processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation to which Marjan is subject, for example the retention of records for tax, anti-money-laundering, or labour-law purposes.

Where the processing is based on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. Withdrawal is given effect in line with Article 09.

Article 06

Sharing and cross-border transfers

Marjan does not sell personal data. Marjan shares personal data only with parties that have a clear role in the purposes described in Article 04 and that are subject to written confidentiality and data-protection obligations.

Personal data may be transferred outside the UAE where Marjan engages a service provider whose infrastructure is located in another jurisdiction, or where a counterparty in a transaction is established outside the UAE. Such transfers are made in accordance with Article 22 of the UAE PDPL and, in particular, are permitted only where:

  • the receiving jurisdiction has been recognised by the UAE Data Office as providing an adequate level of protection;
  • the transfer is governed by binding contractual safeguards approved by the UAE Data Office, including the application of standard data-protection clauses;
  • the transfer is necessary for the performance of a contract concluded in your interest; or
  • you have given your explicit consent to the proposed transfer after being informed of the possible risks.
Article 07

Recipients of personal data

The principal categories of recipient with whom Marjan may share personal data are set out below. In every case, the recipient is bound by written confidentiality and data-protection obligations and is subject to oversight by the Data Protection Officer.

  • Group companies. Subsidiaries and affiliates of Marjan that participate in the development of Al Marjan Island.
  • Government bodies. The RAK Land Department, the RAK Department of Economic Development, the UAE Federal Tax Authority, the UAE Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security, and other authorities to which disclosure is required by law.
  • Service providers. Hosting and cloud-infrastructure providers, customer-relationship-management platforms, email and marketing platforms, identity-verification providers, recruitment platforms, and professional advisers including auditors, lawyers, and tax advisers.
  • Operators and partners. Hotel and branded-residence operators where you elect to be introduced for a specific reservation, residency, or rental-management service.
  • Appointed brokers. Brokers licensed by the RAK Land Department where you elect to be introduced for a specific transaction.
  • Acquirers. In the event of a corporate restructuring, sale, or comparable transaction, a prospective acquirer and its advisers, subject to written confidentiality undertakings.

Article 09

Exercising your rights

Requests to exercise any of the rights described in Article 08 should be addressed to the Data Protection Officer using the contact details in Article 01. To allow Marjan to respond, please include sufficient information to identify yourself and the personal data to which the request relates.

Marjan will acknowledge each request within seven (7) days of receipt and will provide a substantive response within thirty (30) days, in line with Article 23 of the UAE PDPL. Where a request is complex, or where a number of requests have been received from the same person, Marjan may extend the response period by a further thirty (30) days and will notify you of the extension and the reasons for it.

No fee is charged for requests made under the UAE PDPL. Where a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, Marjan may either charge a reasonable fee to cover its administrative costs or decline to act on the request, in each case explaining the basis for the decision.

Article 10

Complaints to the UAE Data Office

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the UAE Data Office at any time if you consider that the processing of your personal data by Marjan infringes the UAE PDPL or any related regulation. Lodging a complaint does not affect any other right of redress that may be available to you under UAE law.

Before submitting a complaint to the UAE Data Office, Marjan invites you to contact the Data Protection Officer at the address set out in Article 01 so that any concern can be addressed directly and, where possible, resolved without the involvement of the supervisory authority.

Updates to this notice

This notice is reviewed at least once a year and is updated when the processing it describes changes in a way that is material to the rights of data subjects. The version number, the effective date, and the date of last review are shown at the top of this page. Material changes are flagged on the homepage of the Website for thirty (30) days following publication.

Contact

For any matter relating to this notice, please contact the Data Protection Officer at dpo@marjan.example, or by post to the Data Protection Officer, Marjan (Master Developer Real Estate L.L.C.), RAK Government Complex, Al Nakheel, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE.
