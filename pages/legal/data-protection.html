Data Protection

How Marjan collects, uses, stores and protects personal data, in line with UAE data protection law.

Effective Date01 January 2026
Last Reviewed01 May 2026
DPO Contactdpo@marjan.ae
Document RefMJN-POL-DP-001
Article 01

Scope

This policy applies to personal data processed by Marjan LLC and its subsidiaries in connection with its business activities. It covers personal data of investors, partners, employees, contractors and members of the public.

Article 02

Data We Collect

Marjan may collect identifying, contact, professional and transactional data necessary for its business activities. The specific categories of data collected depend on the relationship and purpose.

Article 03

Legal Basis

Marjan processes personal data on the basis of consent, contractual necessity, legitimate interest or legal obligation, as applicable. The relevant basis is identified at the point of collection.

Article 04

How We Use Data

Personal data is used for purposes that are explained at the point of collection or as otherwise permitted by law. Uses include client onboarding, investor relations, employment administration, marketing communications and statutory reporting.

Article 05

Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, and in accordance with applicable retention obligations.

Article 06

Your Rights

Individuals have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of their personal data, subject to applicable law. Requests should be sent to dpo@marjan.ae.

Article 07

International Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the United Arab Emirates, Marjan applies appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable law and recognised data-protection standards.

Article 08

Contacting the DPO

Questions about this policy or about Marjan's processing of personal data should be directed to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@marjan.ae. Complaints may also be raised with the relevant UAE supervisory authority.
