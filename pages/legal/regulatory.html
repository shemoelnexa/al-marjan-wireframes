Article 02

Authorised activities

Marjan is authorised under its commercial licence to carry on the following activities, in each case within the territory of the United Arab Emirates and subject to the supervision of the competent authorities described in Article 04.

  • Real-estate development. Master planning, infrastructure delivery, plot release, and project development across the Al Marjan Island freehold area.
  • Real-estate investment. Acquisition and holding of land and built assets within the master plan, structuring of joint ventures with hotel operators and branded-residence partners, and management of investment vehicles described in confidential offering memoranda.
  • Marketing and sales. Direct marketing, promotion, and sale of plots, units, and investment products related to Al Marjan Island, transacted through brokers licensed by the RAK Land Department.
  • Advisory services. Strategic advisory provided to government bodies and partners on matters connected with the development of Al Marjan Island.

Article 04

Regulatory oversight and supervisors

Marjan is subject to the supervision of the following authorities in the conduct of its activities. Disclosures, returns, and statutory filings are made to each authority within the time limits prescribed by the applicable law.

  • Government of Ras Al Khaimah. As ultimate beneficial owner and as the granting authority of the master-development mandate, the Government of Ras Al Khaimah supervises the strategic direction of Marjan through the RAK Investment and Development Office.
  • RAK Department of Economic Development. Supervises Marjan's commercial licence, scope of activities, and corporate filings.
  • RAK Land Department. Supervises the registration of title, plot release, and broker accreditation across the master-plan area.
  • UAE Federal Tax Authority. Supervises Corporate Tax and Value Added Tax compliance under federal law.
  • UAE Central Bank. Where Marjan or its affiliates engage with financial institutions for project finance or treasury services, those institutions are themselves supervised by the UAE Central Bank.
Article 05

Ownership structure and UBO disclosure

In accordance with UAE Cabinet Resolution No. 58 of 2020 on the Regulation of Real Beneficiary Procedures, Marjan maintains an up-to-date register of its ultimate beneficial owners and discloses the chain of ownership to the competent licensing authority on the prescribed schedule.

Ownership chain

  • Marjan (Master Developer Real Estate L.L.C.) is wholly owned, directly or indirectly, by entities under the control of the Government of Ras Al Khaimah.
  • The ultimate beneficial owner, for the purposes of UAE law, is the Government of Ras Al Khaimah, represented by the Ruler's Court and acting through the RAK Investment and Development Office.
  • No natural person holds, directly or indirectly, 25% or more of the issued capital or voting rights of Marjan or of any of its parent entities.

Updates

Any change to the ownership chain is notified to the RAK Department of Economic Development within fifteen (15) days of the change taking effect, and the public summary published on this page is updated at the next periodic review.

Article 07

Foreign ownership rules

Al Marjan Island is a designated freehold area under the legislation of the Emirate of Ras Al Khaimah. Within the master-plan boundary, natural and legal persons of any nationality may acquire freehold title to land and built assets, subject to compliance with the registration requirements of the RAK Land Department and to any sectoral approvals that apply to the relevant asset class.

Foreign investors holding freehold title within Al Marjan Island are entitled to:

  • 100% ownership of the registered property, with full rights of use, lease, sale, and transmission by inheritance.
  • Registration of title in the name of the individual purchaser, a UAE-incorporated company, or, subject to RAK Land Department approval, a qualifying foreign entity.
  • Repatriation of capital and rental income in accordance with the UAE federal framework for the free movement of capital.

Foreign ownership of the underlying corporate vehicles operating commercial businesses on Al Marjan Island is also permitted up to 100%, in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 32 of 2021 on Commercial Companies, as amended.

Article 08

Long-term residency

Investors acquiring property on Al Marjan Island may qualify for the long-term residence permits issued by the UAE Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security under the provisions of UAE Cabinet Resolution No. 65 of 2022. The principal qualifying thresholds are set out below.

Permit category Qualifying property value (minimum) Term
Investor visaAED 750,000 in residential property, registered to the applicant2 years, renewable
Golden Visa (property route)AED 2,000,000 in residential property, registered to the applicant, free or financed through an approved UAE bank10 years, renewable
Retirement visaAED 1,000,000 in property, or comparable savings or income, for applicants aged 55 and over5 years, renewable

Eligibility is determined by the federal authority on the basis of the documentation submitted at the time of application. Marjan does not act as an immigration agent and does not guarantee the issuance of any permit.

Article 09

Statutory disclosures

In accordance with UAE law and good corporate-governance practice, Marjan publishes the following standing disclosures on this page and updates them at each periodic review.

Related-party transactions

Marjan undertakes transactions with entities under the common control of the Government of Ras Al Khaimah, including infrastructure providers, utility companies, and tourism agencies. All such transactions are concluded on arm's length terms, recorded in the statutory books of account, and disclosed to the licensing authority where the prescribed thresholds are exceeded.

Sanctions and restricted dealings

Marjan complies with the sanctions and counter-terrorism financing framework administered by the UAE Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation, and with the targeted financial-sanctions lists issued by the United Nations Security Council. Counterparties are screened against the relevant lists prior to onboarding, and the screening is refreshed at defined intervals throughout the relationship.

Material litigation

Where material litigation or regulatory enforcement is threatened or commenced against Marjan and is required to be disclosed under applicable law, a summary is published on this page within the time limits prescribed by the competent authority.

Article 10

Whistleblower channel

Marjan operates a confidential whistleblower channel for the reporting of suspected breaches of law, regulation, or internal policy, including breaches of the Anti-Corruption Policy described on this site. Reports may be made by any employee, contractor, supplier, broker, customer, or member of the public.

Channels available

  • Email. Reports may be sent to ethics@marjan.example, monitored by the Group Compliance team and the Audit Committee.
  • Postal address. Reports may be addressed to the Audit Committee Chair, Marjan (Master Developer Real Estate L.L.C.), RAK Government Complex, Al Nakheel, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE, marked "Strictly confidential".
  • External counsel. Reports may be made through Marjan's external whistleblowing counsel, the contact details for which are published on the careers and supplier portals.

Non-retaliation

Marjan prohibits retaliation against any person who makes a good-faith report under this channel. Any act of retaliation will itself be treated as a serious breach of internal policy and may give rise to disciplinary action and, where appropriate, referral to the competent authority.

Contact

Formal correspondence on regulatory matters should be directed to legal@marjan.example. Confidential ethics and whistleblowing matters should be directed to ethics@marjan.example. Marjan acknowledges every submission and provides a substantive response within thirty (30) days of receipt.
