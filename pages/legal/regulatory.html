Regulatory Information

Details of the regulatory frameworks under which Marjan operates and the entities responsible for oversight of its developments.

Effective Date01 January 2026
Last Reviewed01 April 2026
RegulatorGovernment of Ras Al Khaimah
Document RefMJN-LEG-002
Article 01

Entity & Registration

Marjan LLC is incorporated in the Emirate of Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. The Company operates under a master developer mandate granted by the Government of Ras Al Khaimah.

Article 02

Regulatory Bodies

Marjan's developments are overseen by the relevant authorities of the Emirate of Ras Al Khaimah, including but not limited to municipal planning, land, environmental, tourism and economic authorities.

Article 03

Compliance Programme

Marjan operates a structured compliance programme covering anti-corruption, sanctions, data protection and operational governance. The programme is reviewed annually and updated as required.

Article 04

Reporting Obligations

Marjan reports periodically to the relevant authorities on programme delivery, environmental performance and economic contribution. Annual reporting is publicly summarised in the Marjan Annual Review.

Article 05

Investor Disclosures

Material information that may be relevant to capital partners is communicated through the Investor Relations function. Investors may request additional disclosures through formal due-diligence channels.
