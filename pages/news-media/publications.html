Annual Review 2025

The Annual Review 2025 documents a year of transformative progress across Marjan's masterplanned programme — from the confirmation of Wynn Al Marjan Island's structural milestone to the close of the workforce housing capital partnership and the launch of RAK Central Phase 1 civic planning. The review presents a measured account of delivery against RAK Vision 2030 targets, with forward guidance on the 2026–2030 pipeline and a detailed sustainability chapter aligned to the UAE Net-Zero by 2050 roadmap.
