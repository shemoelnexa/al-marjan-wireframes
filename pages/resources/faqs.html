Investment

Ticket sizes, structures, the deal process and the underlying returns thesis. Drafted with Marjan's investment team.

What is the minimum ticket size for direct hotel investment on Al Marjan Island?

For direct equity participation in a single-asset hotel JV, Marjan typically engages with capital partners writing AED 75M (c. US$ 20M) and above per asset. Below that threshold we channel interest into our co-investment vehicles, branded-residence pre-sale tranches, or feeder structures alongside our institutional partners.

Land-only acquisitions in the development plots that remain on the masterplan have separate, lower thresholds — set per release and disclosed under NDA.

What's the typical return profile on a Marjan hospitality JV?

Underwriting committed in 2023-2024 has centred on a 14-18% project-level IRR with a 1.9-2.4x equity multiple over a 7-10 year hold, exit assumed via stabilised sale to a regional REIT or to a sovereign-backed institutional buyer.

Net rental yields on branded residences in the same JVs are modelled at 6-8% net of opex and FF&E reserve, supported by the EY 2024 outlook and the Wynn effect built into RevPAR forecasts from 2027 onwards.

How long is the typical investment process from first enquiry to close?

From first enquiry to a signed term sheet is typically 6-10 weeks. From term sheet to financial close is a further 12-16 weeks, dominated by KYC, third-party DD (legal, technical, environmental) and SPA negotiation.

Marjan provides an in-house data room, standardised JV templates and a single point of contact through the investments team to compress this timeline where possible.

What is the Wynn Effect, and how is it modelled into Marjan's projections?

The Wynn Effect is the documented uplift in arrivals, ADR and adjacent asset values that has historically followed the opening of an integrated resort (Macau, Singapore, Las Vegas). For Al Marjan Island, EY modelled a +35-45% RevPAR uplift across the island's hotel inventory in the 24 months post the Wynn Al Marjan Island opening in 2027.

Marjan's underwriting uses a conservative variant of this scenario — a +28% uplift sustained over a five-year window — with a sensitivity case that flexes the timing and depth of the effect.

Is there UAE corporate tax exposure for hotel JVs on Al Marjan Island?

The 9% UAE federal corporate tax (effective June 2023) applies to mainland UAE legal entities with taxable income above AED 375,000. Most operating hotel JVs on Al Marjan are structured through mainland RAK entities and are therefore in-scope, with standard deductibility rules.

Free-zone qualifying activities and qualifying intra-group transactions can benefit from a 0% rate. Marjan's tax counsel walks each investor through the structure decision case-by-case.

Can I leverage my Marjan acquisition with local UAE banks?

Yes. UAE banks routinely lend against income-producing hotel and residential assets at 50-60% LTV for institutional borrowers, and at 70-75% LTV against branded residences for UAE-resident buyers. Indicative interest rates currently sit in the 5.0-6.5% range depending on tenor and borrower profile.

Marjan maintains panel relationships with Emirates NBD, RAK Bank, Mashreq and ADCB and can introduce investors directly into structured-finance teams.

How is foreign exchange and capital repatriation handled?

The UAE dirham is pegged to the US dollar at AED 3.6725 per USD, removing day-to-day FX volatility for dollar-denominated investors. There are no exchange controls and no restrictions on repatriating capital, dividends or sale proceeds.

For non-dollar investors, Marjan can introduce treasury advisors for hedging structures, although the dollar peg means most institutional capital chooses to remain unhedged.

Does Marjan offer co-investment alongside its own equity?

Yes. Marjan typically retains a meaningful equity stake in anchor projects — usually 25-49% — and welcomes co-investment from aligned LPs. For institutional investors writing US$ 50M+ tickets, bespoke side-letter arrangements are available covering governance, exit alignment and ROFR provisions on adjacent plots.

Ownership & Residency

Title, freehold rights, the Golden Visa pathway and the practicalities of holding UAE property as a foreign national.

Can foreign nationals hold freehold title on Al Marjan Island?

Yes. Al Marjan Island is a designated freehold zone under Ras Al Khaimah law. Foreign nationals — individuals and corporates, of any nationality — can hold 100% freehold ownership of land and built property registered with the RAK Land Department, with full inheritance rights.

Title is registered under the RAK title system; original deeds are issued in both Arabic and English and are recognised across all UAE banking and visa processes.

How does the UAE Golden Visa programme work for property investors?

An investor purchasing UAE property with a value of AED 2M (c. US$ 545K) or above qualifies for a renewable 10-year Golden Visa for themselves, their spouse and their children. The investor can sponsor parents and domestic staff under the same visa.

The visa is decoupled from continuous physical presence in the UAE — there is no minimum stay requirement — and confers full residency rights including the ability to open bank accounts, register vehicles, enrol children in schools and access UAE healthcare.

Marjan's residential sales team handles the visa application alongside the property transaction, with typical end-to-end processing of 4-6 weeks from title transfer.

Is there any property tax, capital gains tax or inheritance tax in the UAE?

The UAE levies no annual property tax, no capital gains tax and no inheritance tax on individuals. A one-time 4% transfer fee is paid to the RAK Land Department on the registration of title (typically shared between buyer and seller, though commercially negotiable).

VAT at 5% applies to most commercial property transactions but generally does not apply to first sales of residential property.

What does the ownership structure look like for a branded residence?

A branded residence on Al Marjan Island — e.g. Anantara Mina Al Arab, Marriott Residences — is sold as a freehold unit registered to the buyer at the RAK Land Department. The owner separately enters into a long-form management agreement with the hotel operator (typically 10-20 years, automatically renewable) under which the operator services the unit, manages any rental programme and maintains brand standards.

Owners can occupy the unit personally, place it into the operator's rental pool, or operate it independently subject to brand-standard maintenance requirements.

What ongoing service charges apply to Al Marjan residential property?

Service charges vary by community and asset class. As an indicative range: standard freehold apartments AED 14-22 per sqft annually; mid-market branded residences AED 22-32 per sqft; ultra-prime beachfront branded residences AED 38-55 per sqft. Charges cover building services, common-area maintenance, security, district cooling and, for branded residences, the operator service overlay.

All charges are regulated and capped under RAK community-management frameworks.

Are short-term holiday rentals permitted on Al Marjan Island?

Yes. RAK Tourism Authority operates a holiday-home licensing regime; individual unit owners can list on Airbnb, Booking.com and equivalent platforms under a paid annual permit. Branded residences usually channel short-stay activity through the operator's central reservations under the rental pool agreement.

Can a UAE company hold property on my behalf?

Yes. A RAK Economic Zone or RAK ICC offshore company — both available with 100% foreign ownership — can hold title to Al Marjan property. This structure is common for investors managing portfolios, planning succession, or seeking corporate-level confidentiality.

Marjan can introduce accredited corporate-services providers to set up the holding entity in parallel with the property transaction.

Hospitality & Operators

The anchor openings, the operator roster, and the commercial models Marjan supports for hospitality partners.

When does Wynn Al Marjan Island open, and what does it bring?

Wynn Al Marjan Island is scheduled to open in early 2027. It comprises 1,542 keys across hotel, suites and villas, a 224,000 sqft gaming floor, 22 restaurants, a 1,500-seat theatre and a 5,000-seat events arena.

It is a US$ 3.9B development and the first integrated resort to be licenced for gaming in the UAE — the catalyst for the island-wide RevPAR uplift modelled into Marjan's investment thesis from 2027 onwards.

When does Hard Rock Hotel Al Marjan Island open?

Hard Rock Hotel Al Marjan Island is scheduled for soft opening in late 2026, ahead of the Wynn opening. The asset comprises 657 keys including 39 suites and 5 signature villas, alongside a 4,000-capacity entertainment venue and a Hard Rock Cafe flagship.

Which operators are already active on Al Marjan Island?

Open and operating today: Rixos Bab Al Bahr, Movenpick Resort, DoubleTree by Hilton, Bab Al Bahr Residences. Confirmed and under construction: Hard Rock Hotel (2026), Wynn Al Marjan Island (2027), JW Marriott Resort, Nikki Beach Resort & Spa, Anantara Mina Al Arab Residences, Marriott Residences. The combined operator roster represents around 6,000 additional keys in the pipeline against today's c. 3,500 operating keys.

What operating models does Marjan support for hospitality partners?

Marjan structures hospitality assets under any of four models, selected per project based on operator preference, capital structure and risk appetite:

Hotel Management Agreement (HMA) — operator runs the asset on behalf of Marjan/JV ownership; typical base fee 2-3% of revenue, incentive fee 8-12% of GOP.
Franchise — Marjan or appointed third-party operator runs day-to-day; brand licence with royalties of 4-6% of room revenue.
Lease — operator pays a fixed or hybrid (fixed + variable) rent for full operating control.
Owner-operate with brand licence — used for select Marjan-led concepts and bespoke standalone venues.

How big is the current operating hospitality base, and what's the supply pipeline?

As of Q4 2024, Al Marjan Island has c. 3,500 operating keys. Confirmed and announced pipeline adds c. 6,000 keys, with stabilised inventory around 9,500 keys by 2028. Colliers' 2024 supply analysis benchmarks this against forecast demand and shows an absorption surplus through 2029 — the basis on which Marjan continues to back further anchor openings.

What ADR and RevPAR ranges are currently being achieved?

Year-to-date 2024 system ADR on Al Marjan Island has averaged AED 720-880 across the operating set, with occupancy of 78-85% and a blended RevPAR of AED 580-740. The EY 2024 outlook projects ADR growth of 6-9% per annum to 2027, accelerating to 12-18% post-Wynn.

How does staffing and accommodation work for hospitality operators?

Marjan estimates total operator staffing across the stabilised island at c. 7,200 hospitality FTEs by 2028. Dedicated staff accommodation plots have been carved out on the mainland adjacent to Al Marjan, with two Marjan-developed staff villages already operational and a third under construction.

Operators can either lease beds from the Marjan-developed accommodation or develop their own under a long-term land lease — both routes are available.

Partners, Vendors & General

Becoming a Marjan vendor, working with the design and construction supply chain, and general enquiries.

How do I become an accredited vendor or supplier to Marjan?

Vendor onboarding is handled through the Marjan procurement portal. The standard pack requires a valid UAE trade licence (or equivalent home-jurisdiction credentials for international vendors), VAT registration if applicable, KYC documentation, insurance certificates and an ESG questionnaire.

Typical processing time from a complete pack is 10-15 working days. Once onboarded, vendors receive direct visibility of tender opportunities across Marjan's development programme.

Does Marjan use a preferred contractor list for construction?

Yes. Marjan maintains a pre-qualified panel of main contractors across categories (high-rise residential, hospitality, marine works, infrastructure). New entrants to the panel are admitted on the basis of demonstrated UAE delivery, financial standing, ESG performance and reference projects.

Sub-contractors and specialist trades are tendered project-by-project with the appointed main contractor.

What is the trade licence framework on Al Marjan Island for an operator's commercial activity?

Commercial operating entities on Al Marjan Island are licensed by the RAK Department of Economic Development for mainland activities, or by RAKEZ for free-zone activities. F&B, retail and hospitality typically use mainland licences; technology, consulting and holding structures often use RAKEZ.

Marjan's commercial team coordinates licence applications alongside lease execution to align openings with statutory readiness.

How do media and press enquiries work?

All press enquiries are routed through the Marjan communications team at press@marjan.ae. Journalists can request the Press Photography Kit, the Annual Report, project renders and on-site filming permits. Press events are listed under News & Media; embargoed information is available to accredited media on request under standard embargo terms.

What ESG and sustainability commitments does Marjan operate under?

Marjan publishes an annual Sustainability Report aligned to GRI standards and the UAE Net-Zero 2050 framework. Current commitments cover marine ecology preservation, district cooling for all new developments, renewable energy procurement targets, single-use plastics elimination across operating tenants, and inclusion targets for the construction supply chain.
