Frequently Asked

Common questions about Marjan's developments, investment process, hospitality partnerships and Ras Al Khaimah.

01

Investment

Ticket sizes, structures, sectors and timelines for investing with Marjan.

What is Marjan's investment minimum?

Investment thresholds vary by sector and structure. Direct co-investments typically start at US$25M, with smaller structures available for specific programmes.

Which sectors does Marjan invest in?

Marjan's investment activity spans four primary sectors: Residential, Hospitality, Commercial and Staff Accommodation. Each sector carries distinct return profiles, hold horizons and capital structures tailored to the underlying demand driver.

How long is Marjan's typical hold horizon?

Hold periods vary by sector, typically ranging from 5 to 15 years. Hospitality co-investments are typically underwritten over 8–10 years; residential and commercial assets may be structured with shorter or longer horizons depending on the deal.

Is currency exposure available beyond AED?

Most Marjan investments are denominated in AED, which is pegged to the US dollar at AED 3.6725 per USD, providing effective USD stability. USD-denominated structures are also available for certain programmes where investor preference or institutional requirements warrant it.

How long does the investment process take?

From first enquiry to financial close, the typical cycle runs 12–16 weeks. This covers initial screening, data room access, due diligence, term sheet negotiation and legal close. Marjan provides an in-house data room and single point of contact to compress this timeline where possible.

02

Development

Marjan's mandate, masterplans and delivery programme.

What is Marjan's relationship to the Ras Al Khaimah government?

Marjan holds a master developer mandate from the Government of Ras Al Khaimah. It is the strategic real-estate arm responsible for planning, enabling and delivering the Emirate's landmark mixed-use destinations, aligned with RAK Vision 2030.

Which masterplans is Marjan delivering?

Marjan's active masterplan programme includes Al Marjan Island, RAK Central and Marjan Beach District. Each masterplan integrates hospitality, residential, commercial, retail and public realm within a long-range spatial framework.

When will RAK Central be complete?

RAK Central is being delivered in phases, with completion across the full masterplan scheduled between 2026 and 2032. Individual district and plot releases are sequenced to match infrastructure readiness and market absorption.

Does Marjan develop infrastructure?

Yes. Marjan's mandate covers integrated infrastructure delivery including mobility networks, utilities, public realm and coastal works. Infrastructure is delivered ahead of or concurrently with built development to ensure each destination is fully serviced at opening.

03

Working with Marjan

Operator partnerships, careers, press and newsletter enquiries.

How can hospitality operators partner with Marjan?

Marjan works with operators across a range of structures — Hotel Management Agreements, franchise, lease and owner-operate models. Operators interested in partnership should submit an Expression of Interest via the Hospitality section, or contact operators@marjan.ae directly.

Does Marjan accept open applications for careers?

Yes. Marjan accepts speculative applications alongside advertised vacancies. Send your CV and covering note to careers@marjan.ae, noting the area of expertise and seniority level you are targeting. Current openings are listed in the Careers section.

How can press contact Marjan?

All press and media enquiries are routed through the Marjan communications team at press@marjan.ae. Journalists can request the Press Kit, renders, project fact sheets and filming permits. Accredited media can access embargoed materials under standard terms. See the Media Centre for press releases and assets.

How can I subscribe to press releases?

Newsletter and press release subscriptions are managed through the Latest News page. Use the subscription form at the foot of the Latest News page to opt in to Marjan's investor updates, project announcements and media releases.
