Methodology & disclaimer

How to read these numbers.

The calculator runs a deterministic discounted-cashflow model over the hold period for each scenario. Inputs default to Marjan's central-case assumptions, sourced and documented in the register above. All results are project-level (pre-fund-level fees), expressed in AED, and assume the AED/USD peg holds for the duration of the modelled hold.

Ranges should be read as plausible bands, not single-point forecasts. Macroeconomic conditions, the precise opening trajectory of Wynn Al Marjan Island and the absorption rate of competing supply will all move the result. Marjan publishes a quarterly recalibration note alongside the Q1/Q4 investor updates.

This calculator is provided for indicative purposes only. It does not constitute investment advice, an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy securities or property. Investors should conduct their own due diligence and seek independent professional advice before committing capital.
